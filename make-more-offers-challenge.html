Fit

Good fit, or skip it?

Likely a good fit if

  • You already sell a product or service.
  • You can attend five live sessions.
  • You want to practise making offers, not only read about it.

Skip it for now if

  • You want done-for-you income.
  • You cannot attend live. The VIP refund depends on it.
  • You need guaranteed results.

Questions

Quick answers

When does the Make More Offers Challenge open again?

No date is published. The official page says the challenge is paused and offers a waitlist. Our alert sends one email when it reopens.

How much does the challenge cost?

In the last published round: General Admission $97, VIP $297, and Platinum as a $500 upgrade on VIP.

Can I get a refund?

General Admission: no. VIP: only after the challenge, if you attended every day with your camera on and did the homework (conditions explained). Platinum: no.

Is it live or recorded?

It was announced as live online sessions over five days. The VIP refund depends on live attendance.
