Fit

Good fit, or skip it?

The publisher describes the book as business mastery lessons from a roundtable, covering sales, marketing and pricing.

Likely a good fit if

  • You want a low-cost look at Myron Golden's approach before spending more.
  • You prefer reading or listening at your own pace.

Skip it for now if

  • You need feedback on your own business. A book cannot give that.
  • You live outside the US and the $24.99 shipping outweighs the value for you.
