Fit

Add the audio, or skip it?

The audiobook covers the same book, read by the author. You get it by email right after the order, while the printed copy still has to ship.

Likely worth adding if

  • You listen more than you read, for example while driving.
  • You order from outside the US and want something to start with before the book arrives.

Skip it if

  • You only want audio. The official page does not sell it without the book.
  • You plan to buy Mind Over Money Mastery. That bundle already lists the book.
