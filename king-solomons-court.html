Fit

Apply, or skip it?

Applying makes sense if

  • Your business already earns $10,000 to $50,000 a month.
  • You are an author, speaker, coach or consultant with an audience you want to scale.
  • You are comfortable sharing income figures before you know the price.

Skip it for now if

  • You are below the stated revenue range.
  • You need a price, a length and refund terms in writing before you apply.
  • You are looking for a way to start a business. The page says it is not for a quick fix.
