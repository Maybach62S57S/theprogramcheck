How we check

We report what the official pages say, with a link and a date for every fact. We do not publish testimonials, earnings figures or opinions dressed up as experience.

Our process for every brief

  1. We open the official sales page, checkout page and policy pages directly.
  2. We record price, payment options, shipping, refund rules and listed contents, each with its source link and check date.
  3. We say who a program likely fits and who should skip it, based on those terms.
  4. We re-check every brief at least monthly, and on the day a program opens or closes.
  5. When something changes, we update the brief and log the change below.
