What nobody outside can check

Income and results claims in sales material, such as "thousands" helped or a participant earning $24,000 by Wednesday, come without data. Treat them as marketing. The same goes for "total value" figures on sales pages.

Four questions before you buy

  1. Is there a refund option for this exact product? Rules by product
  2. What is the full price, including shipping or installments?
  3. Can you start with something cheaper first, such as a book?
