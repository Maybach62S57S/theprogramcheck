Before you pay

Three checks that save money

  1. Read the checkout total. International shipping is $24.99 on the books.
  2. Untick add-ons you do not want. A ticket bought by accident has a narrow refund window.
  3. For VIP, plan to attend all five days live with your camera on, or assume no refund.
