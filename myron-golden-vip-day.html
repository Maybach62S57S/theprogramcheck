Fit

Apply, or skip it?

Applying makes sense if

  • You already run a business with revenue, and a large investment would not strain it.
  • You want direct time with Myron Golden rather than recorded training.
  • You are ready to discuss the price on a call and walk away if it does not fit.

Skip it for now if

  • You cannot risk losing the money. Assume no refund unless you get other terms in writing.
  • You want a public price before you share your details.
  • You are at an early stage. A book ($29.95) or Golden OPS ($999) has a public price.
