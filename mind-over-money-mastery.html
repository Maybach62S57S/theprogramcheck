Questions

Quick answers

Can I pay Mind Over Money Mastery in installments?

The sales page shows 2 payments of $597 as an exit offer when you leave the page. The main offer is $4,997 in one payment.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

The sales page states none. The official return policy says returns are not accepted, with listed exceptions.

What happens if I miss a payment?

The return policy says a missed installment voids the purchase and paid installments are not refunded.
