Fit

Good fit, or skip it?

Likely a good fit if

  • You want to learn affiliate selling and can lose $999 without strain.
  • You are ready to market products consistently yourself.

Skip it for now if

  • You need a refund option.
  • You expect income because of the 70% claim. Most income depends on your own traffic and sales.
  • You are not sure yet. A book costs under $55 delivered.
